SECUREPATENT

Public knowledge assistant

Educational answers are copied from reviewer-signed bundles. This process cannot open the restricted patent store.

Approved public knowledge only. A refusal does not confirm or deny that a record exists.

How an answer is allowed

  1. The question is matched to a public topic.
  2. One signed bundle of fragments is selected.
  3. The reply quotes those fragments and their citations.
  4. Requests outside the approved set get one fixed reply.
Public process on port 8000. It reads public.db and does not query external patent sites.
